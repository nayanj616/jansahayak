# Scheme research notes

## Existing records materially updated in this pass

- **CSSS (PM-USP):** updated from current 2025–26 Ministry FAQ. Amount, income cap, merit/renewal conditions, NSP route and documents are cited to FAQ sections. The 2025–26 portal window is historical; no 2026–27 application notice was confirmed.
- **Kisan Credit Card:** added DFS and RBI official evidence. The amount remains unknown because a DFS scheme page states an older ₹3 lakh cap while a 2025 Budget FAQ describes a planned rise to ₹5 lakh; the current operative implementation circular was not established. Universal document lists were removed because the RBI framework leaves details to bank policy.
- **Gaon Ki Beti:** MP CM Helpline scheme details and current MP portal route added. ₹500/₹750 monthly benefit tracks, rural/Class XII criteria and application facts cite state sources. 2025–26 portal notice was closed by its August 2026 dates; 2026–27 dates remain unknown.
- **MP ST Student Welfare:** retained scheme-specific claims from the official summary but added MPTAAS portal source for profile/application/status functions; current rate and rules still need a primary scheme notification.
- **MP Kalyani Sahayata:** official MP eService directory confirms the service listing; detailed amounts/eligibility remain supported only by the official myScheme summary and are flagged for rule verification.
- **PMSS for State/UT Police wards:** added Ministry of Home Affairs primary guidelines for award rates and process. No 2026–27 application notice was confirmed.
- **PM Shram Yogi Maandhan:** replaced summary-only reliance with the official Maandhan portal for entry age, income threshold, matching contributions and pension route. Contribution varies by joining age.
- **PM Vishwakarma:** added 2025 MSME booklet and FAQ evidence for training, toolkit, credit, digital incentive, age, exclusions and verification. Loans and later incentives remain conditional on milestones and lender approval.
- **PM-JAY:** added NHA senior-citizen FAQ for age-70+ entitlement. `age_min` and `income_limit` stay null because the standard beneficiary-list route and age-70+ route differ; the flat filters cannot represent both without misleading users.

## Six additions

- **ST Post-Matric Scholarship:** MoTA scheme feature page supports recognized courses from Class X+, ST status, ₹2.5 lakh parental income cap, fee limit and ₹230–₹1,200 monthly maintenance. MP application goes through state portal/NSP; exact current upload checklist and 2026–27 dates remain unknown.
- **ST Pre-Matric Scholarship (IX–X):** MoTA page supports class, income and rate rules; its 2022 guidelines name 2021–22 to 2025–26 while official 2025–26 sanctions show MP operation. 2026–27 continuation/current window needs confirmation.
- **PM-Vidyalaxmi:** Ministry guidelines define QHEI admission, the loan product, income-limited interest subvention, exclusions and digital route. The current portal FAQ supplies the upload checklist, Aadhaar requirement, loan/status/grievance workflows and a claim-submission notice for FY 2025–26 (1 Sep–31 Dec 2026). The current annual QHEI list and any FY 2026–27 interest-claim notice remain to be checked. The FAQ broad feature text says full subvention under an income threshold, but the detailed Ministry guideline places full subvention under separate PM-USP CSIS for specified technical/professional courses; this record follows the detailed guideline and documents the discrepancy.
- **Pratibha Kiran:** MP CM Helpline and Khargone district government page support the urban BPL girl student/Class XII pathway and online portal; the later district page corroborates ₹500/month for 10 months. The older CM Helpline detail also lists a higher technical/medical rate; current validity of that rate is unresolved.
- **MMVY:** MP CM Helpline supports course-specific criteria, tuition reimbursement and application documents. Its scheme-detail data was last updated 2022; the 2025 corrigendum PDF URL on the state portal was inaccessible to the research browser. Exact current rules must be checked before personalized guidance.
- **PMGKAY:** DFPD official page supports five-year free-grain continuation from 2024, NFSA AAY/PHH eligibility and monthly quantities. This is an existing NFSA entitlement delivered through PDS, not a separate new application.
