# Candidate review

Both source inputs were inspected: the friend’s ZIP knowledge base (manifest/readme, schema, sample records, Madhya Pradesh candidates and imported eligibility conventions) and the prior standalone 57-record dataset. The ZIP described approximately 1,978 entries and marked them imported-unverified; its rules-derived eligibility fields were not carried into final records. The full ZIP was not independently researched record by record.

The final dataset contains **44 distinct records**: 15 central, 19 Madhya Pradesh state, and 10 central/state implementation records. The target of 50–60 was not met. Candidate selection prioritized current official identity, meaningful benefit and eligibility evidence, document information where specified, and a usable application route.

`excluded-unresolved-candidates.json` contains 30 candidate entries that were excluded, not screened individually, or require further research. Some source-dataset candidates are represented by a final record under a normalized/combined scheme ID and are omitted from exclusions. Omission does not imply that any citizen is ineligible.
